# Photos needed for carlossimoni.com

Fourteen photo slots on the site, every one currently showing a placeholder.
Drop a file in `public/images/` and set its `src` in `content/photos.ts` to
make a placeholder disappear — no other code changes needed.

| # | What it's for | What to get | Suggested size |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero background (big banner behind the headline + sign-up form) | Wide, landscape shot of a recognizable Tampa Bay scene (skyline, Bayshore Blvd, a palm-lined waterfront). Sits behind a dark overlay with white text on top, so avoid a busy, high-contrast center — open sky/water toward the upper-left reads best. Carlos can be in it but doesn't have to be. | ~1920×1080px |
| 2 | Social share image | Landscape photo used when the link is shared in texts/social — can reuse the hero shot cropped wide. | 1200×630px |
| 3 | South Tampa | A recognizable South Tampa street or Bayshore Blvd shot. | ~800×600px |
| 4 | Westchase | Westchase community entrance, a park, or typical street. | ~800×600px |
| 5 | New Tampa / Wesley Chapel | A newer-build community street. | ~800×600px |
| 6 | Carrollwood | Lakes, established tree-lined streets. | ~800×600px |
| 7 | St. Petersburg / Clearwater | A beach or downtown St. Pete shot. | ~800×600px |
| 8 | Brandon / Riverview | A family-suburb street or community amenity. | ~800×600px |
| 9 | Sarasota / North Port | Downtown Sarasota or a North Port street. | ~800×600px |
| 10 | Spring Hill | A typical Spring Hill (Hernando County) street. | ~800×600px |
| 11 | Lakeland | Downtown Lakeland's lakefront (Lake Mirror / Lake Morton). | ~800×600px |
| 12 | Plant City | Downtown Plant City — small-town main street feel. | ~800×600px |
| 13 | Lakewood Ranch | A community entrance, Main Street, or lake/park amenity — polished, master-planned feel. | ~800×600px |
| 14 | FishHawk Ranch / Valrico | A family-suburb street, top-rated-schools feel. | ~800×600px |
| 15 | "How it works" process photo | Carlos on his phone/laptop, representing a call with a client — can be a staged, candid-style shot. | ~700×500px |

**Easiest path:** one short photo session covers #1, #2, and #15 (just Carlos,
different poses/crops). The twelve neighborhood photos (#3–#14) can be taken
over a couple of weekends driving around — Sarasota/North Port, Spring Hill,
Lakeland, Plant City, and Lakewood Ranch are each a longer drive from Tampa
than the first six, so those may be worth a separate trip. Stock photos of
each area work as a temporary substitute if that's faster; swap them for
real ones once Carlos is out shooting content anyway (same trip as the video
scripts in `docs/marketing/scripts/` back in the CMADraft repo).
